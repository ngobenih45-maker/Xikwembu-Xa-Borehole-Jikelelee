Who we are

Water drilling you can trust

We are a water-drilling company based in Bungeni, Makhado, serving communities and businesses in Magoro, Malamulele, Elim, Mamaila, Mbokota, Ka-Bungeni, Giyani, Phalaborwa, Tzaneen and Makhado.

We offer professional borehole drilling, geophysical surveys, pump installation and complete water solutions – with transparent pricing, free transport and free surveying on every job.

We are open 24/7 for inquiries and bookings, so water is only a message away.
