X Xikwembu xa Borehole Jikelelee is a water drilling service based in Bungeni, Makhado, Limpopo. We support customers looking for borehole drilling and practical water solutions for their homes, farms and businesses.

We believe customers deserve clear information, responsive communication and a service approach that takes their needs seriously. From an initial enquiry and free surveying to discussing drilling options, we aim to make the process straightforward.

Every site is different. Contact us to discuss your location, required depth and water needs so we can guide you on the next step.
